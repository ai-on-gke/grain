`acme/widgets`' [`.grain/workflows/ci.yml`](2026-11-03-capabilities-ci-repo/acme-widgets-ci.yml) has these jobs, all with `cache: false`:

- `read-other`: declares `repo#read={"repo":"other/repo"}` and clones it.
- `write-other`: declares `repo#write={"repo":"other/repo"}`, clones it and pushes `refs/heads/candidate-<time>`.
- `publish`: has `package-publish` from the repo's `ci_capabilities`. It asks the refresh route three times, 20 s apart, then asks `docker-credential-grain get`.
- `undeclared`: declares nothing and clones `other/repo`.
- `legacy-read`: declares `repo-read:other/repo` and clones it.

Runs are started with [`ci-run.sh <event>`](2026-11-03-capabilities-ci-repo/ci-run.sh) (`POST /api/ci/runs`) and read with [`ci-show.py`](2026-11-03-capabilities-ci-repo/ci-show.py).

The picker says `CI only: not a grant for <kind>; a CI job declares it in its workflow's capabilities:` and links to the docs' Repository access section. Tests: `TestCapabilityPolicyEnforcesCIOnlyForRepoAccessGrants` (`pkg/model/capability_ref_test.go`, new loop), `GrantsPicker.test.jsx`, and `capability-policy-pickers.spec.js`, which now also shoots light and dark. |
