## Stubs

1. **Board rows posted to `POST /api/boards`.** In the UI a board is made by
   filing a task whose pull request against the team's config repository adds
   the row; the demo cannot merge that pull request. Boards for `qa-infra` were
   therefore posted straight to the API (as `e2e/teams-walkthrough.spec.js`
   already does). Everything after that, from listing and opening the boards to
   switching teams and filing "Add panel" tasks, went through the UI.
2. **A few setup and verification calls went to grain's own API** from the
   same browser context: `GET /api/teams`, `/api/tasks/:id`, `/api/schedules`,
   `/api/console-sessions` and `/api/boards`, to confirm what the UI had saved.
   On the re-verification server, a team and a task were also created with
   `POST`. No grain response was mocked.

Nothing else was stubbed. There was no GitHub and no live agent session; the
demo's own fake state stood in for them.

## Findings

### F1: a team's board opened under another team showed that team's tasks (fixed)
**Reproduction** (demo server, nothing stubbed but the board row):
1. Create team `qa-infra`, file a task on it, and `POST /api/boards
   {"name":"QA Board","kind":"list","team":"qa-infra"}`.
2. Open `/teams/default/boards/<id>`, or open `/teams/qa-infra/boards/<id>` and
   pick `admin` in the team selector.

**Before:** "QA Board" lists the default team's backlog and not qa-infra's
task, although `/teams/default/boards` does not list that board at all. Rows
ticked there never reached the batch bar, because the selection was resolved
against the picked team's list.

**Expected and now:** the board draws its own team's tasks, carries a
"QA Infra's board" chip, and its rows can be selected for batch actions. Under
the board's own team the chip is not shown. Under All teams the board still
draws every team's tasks, unchanged.

**Fix:** `ui/src/App.jsx` derives `openBoardTasks` (the open board's own team's
tasks when another specific team is selected) and feeds it to every board pane
and to `selectedTasks`. `ui/src/components/BoardPage.jsx` shows the chip.

**Regression:** `ui/e2e/team-board-off-team.spec.js` (real demo server). It
failed on the base commit and passes with the fix.

**On `main`:** this fix is on `candidate` only. `main` has no All teams view
(grain/task-3088), switching teams closes an open board, and a link to a board
under another team moves the page to the board's own team. So the bug cannot
happen there. The port to `main` leaves out the `App.jsx`/`BoardPage.jsx`
change. Its copy of the spec checks `main`'s behaviour instead: the link
follows the board to `/t/<team>/boards/<id>`, the page shows that team's task,
and the task's row can be selected.

**Tests run:** that spec; `boards.spec.js`, `boards_workflows.spec.js`,
`teams-walkthrough.spec.js`, `cross-team-deep-links.spec.js` and
`team-navigation-scoping.spec.js` (25 passed); vitest `src/App*`,
`BoardsPage`, `BulkEditOverlay` (202 passed).

### F2: deleting a team leaves its boards, templates and suites behind (follow-up)
`model.Store.DeleteTeam` refuses a team that still has tasks or schedules, but
never looks at `board`, `template` or `suite`, all of which carry a `team`
column. In the walkthrough, `qa-infra` was deleted while `board-5` still
belonged to it. The board stayed in `/api/boards` as `team: "qa-infra"`: listed
under All teams, under no team at all, and inherited by whoever next creates a
team with the ID `qa-infra`, since deleting a team frees its ID (the same
hazard `dropCapabilityTeam` exists for). Deciding between refusing, as for
schedules, and cascading or reassigning needs a decision about deletion
semantics, so it is filed as a follow-up rather than fixed here.

### Observations (not filed)
- **O1:** auto-generated slugs drop non-ASCII letters (`Équipe Ünicode` →
  `quipe-nicode`) instead of transliterating them.
- **O2:** the delete confirm names the team ID, not its display name.
- **O3:** after choosing a team from the sidebar selector with the keyboard,
  focus is lost to `<body>`.
- The report board kind is scoped by its query only (`GET /api/metrics`), not
  by team. I did not exercise it under a team in this run.
- The Task Detail Repo select for a qa-infra task offers `acme/widgets` but not
  `grain/config-qa-infra`, while New task offers the config repo. This is a
  minor inconsistency and I did not chase it.
