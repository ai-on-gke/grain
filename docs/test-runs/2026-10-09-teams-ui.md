## Executive Summary

This walkthrough thoroughly exercised the complete multi-team lifecycle in a real browser session against a live `grain demo` backend server:
1. **Team Settings & Inspection:** Inspected team cards, chips, concurrency limits, system default badges, and deletion safeguards.
2. **Team Creation & Validation:** Validated input sanitization, required fields, slug formatting rules, numeric bounds (preventing negative or fractional limits), duplicate detection (case-insensitive), and automatic slug generation from team names.
3. **Team Editing & Safeguards:** Edited existing team limits, time zones, default models, and descriptions while verifying administrative safeguards preventing removal of all team admins/members.
4. **Global Team Selector & Persistence:** Tested global team filtering across tasks, sessions, schedules, and boards, verifying `localStorage` persistence under `grain.selectedTeam`.
5. **Task Intake, Detail Overlays & Bulk Edit:** Filed tasks targeted to specific teams, verified detail overlay chips and inline team reassignment, and executed bulk-edit team reassignment across multiple tasks.
6. **Schedules & Console Sessions:** Verified team scoping in automated schedule creation with team default time-zone inheritance, and verified team scoping for standing interactive console sessions.
7. **Multi-Team Saved Boards:** Created custom boards scoped to a team, verified list visibility and filtering, and validated that "New board" / "Add panel" tasks correctly route to the team's configuration repository (`grain/config-<team>`).
8. **Deletion Safeguards:** Confirmed that teams with active tasks cannot be deleted, verified deletion protection on the default team, and verified clean deletion once tasks, schedules, and sessions are reassigned or removed.

## Findings & Edge Behaviors Verified

- **Input Sanitization & Whitespace:** Leading and trailing whitespace on slug inputs is cleanly trimmed before validation.
- **Slug Normalization:** Auto-generated slugs convert uppercase letters to lowercase, spaces and special characters to hyphens, and strip non-alphanumeric punctuation.
- **Validation Bounds:** Limits must be non-negative integers. Negative numbers and floats/decimals are correctly rejected on both frontend and backend.
- **Integrity Safeguards:** Teams with active tasks, attached schedules, or open sessions cannot be deleted. Deletion of the default team is prohibited.
- **Context Routing:** Board management and team config actions are properly isolated and scoped to `grain/config-<team>`.
- **No Stubs:** All interactions were performed against live server endpoints and evaluated using actual browser DOM rendering.
