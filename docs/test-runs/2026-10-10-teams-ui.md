## Findings & Edge Behaviors Verified

- **Input Sanitization & Boundary Rules:** Leading and trailing whitespace on slug inputs is trimmed. Slugs must start and end with an alphanumeric character; hyphens at boundaries (`qa-`, `-qa`) are rejected.
- **Resource Limits Validation:** `teamLimitsError` enforces whole numbers `>= 0`. Negative integers (`-1`) and decimals (`1.5`) are blocked before submission.
- **Inherited Team Defaults:** Creating a schedule under a team with a configured `timeZone` (`Europe/Berlin`) automatically populates the schedule's timezone input.
- **Administrative Access Safeguards:** Teams cannot be stripped of all administrators and members during edits.
- **Destructive Action Safety:** Browser confirmation dialogs protect against accidental team deletions; cancelling preserves team state.
- **Relational Deletion Integrity:** Teams with active task references cannot be deleted until tasks are reassigned or deleted. The system default team cannot be deleted under any circumstances.
- **Configuration Repository Routing:** Board creation and panel addition tasks automatically target `grain/config-<team>`, isolating board configurations per team.
