## 2. Walkthrough

### 2.1 Cross-tab pins (#538) — held

`ui/e2e/sidebar-pinned-cross-tab-walkthrough.spec.js` (new) drives one
browser context at 1440×900 with two pages, and neither page is ever
reloaded:

1. In tab A, Customize sidebar → pin *Settings → General* and *Settings →
   Git* → Save. Tab B shows both, General above Git.
2. In tab A, **Move up** on Git → Save. Tab B redraws with Git above
   General, compared by the bounding boxes' `y`.
3. In tab A, Customize General → **Color Red** → Save. Tab B's General icon
   turns `rgb(239, 68, 68)`, which it was not before.
4. A third tab opened now shows Git above General, with General red.
5. In tab A, **Reset to default** → Save. All three tabs drop General and
   Git and keep Inbox.

The existing `sidebar-pinned-cross-tab.spec.js` (pin, then a removed key)
passes too. Hidden sidebar sections follow other tabs through the same
`storage` listener (`sidebarSections.js:112`).

### 2.2 Contrast, measured (#509, #521) — F1 and F2, fixed

`2026-11-03-ui-fixes/contrast.mjs` runs axe-core's `color-contrast` rule
at 1440×900 in light and dark over 23 pages:

- Inbox and the kanban board (`/boards/board-1`);
- the report (`/boards/board-3`) and a task page (`/tasks/8`);
- Health (`/system/health`);
- every Settings section: models, sandboxes, credentials, access,
  hostds, preferences, upgrade, caches, engine, config-repo, general, git,
  repositories, tasks, agents, capabilities, ci and memory.

The theme is set through `grain.themeMode` as well as
`prefers-color-scheme`. Axe can leave a node undetermined, for example a
background it cannot resolve. For those nodes the script falls back to
`getComputedStyle`: the text colour times every ancestor's opacity, over
the ancestors' backgrounds composited in order.

**Every text node under 4.5:1, before the fix** (`contrast-before.json`,
4,172 nodes checked):

| Node | Pages | Theme | Nodes | Ratio | Colours | Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Build stamp `a[data-testid=build-stamp]` (`v0.0.0-…-3d811a7d`) | all 23 | light | 23 | 4.27 | `#7d7a74` on `#ffffff` | 10.88px |
| Report thin percentiles, `td > span[aria-label^="Only "]` (`1h 24m*`, `8m 0s*`, `10m 0s*`, `1h 0m*`) | `/boards/board-3` | light | 10 | 2.64 | `#999997` on `#f7f6f3` | 14px |
| — | all 23 | dark | 0 | — | — | — |

**After the fix** (`contrast-after.json`): there are **0 nodes under
4.5:1** in either theme. Of the 212 nodes axe left undetermined (120
`bgOverlap`, 78 `bgGradient` and 14 others), the fallback measured 118,
and none of those was under AA either. The other 94 have no text of their
own or no selector the fallback can query. The `keep_running` chip (MUI
outlined `warning`) is on the Inbox page measured here, and it passes.

The regression guard is `ui/e2e/text-contrast-axe.spec.js` (new). It runs
axe over the Inbox, the seeded report (with its thin cells on screen) and
Preferences, in both themes, and expects no violations. On the base build
it would report F1 on all three pages and F2 on the report.

### 2.3 `keep_running` marker (#466) — held

The `keep-running-badge.spec.js` from #466 covers a held `keep_running`
proposal at 1440 and 390 in light and dark. It passes in all four. On the
Inbox row (beside Approve) and on the board card, the badge reads "keeps
running". Its `title` says why: "keep_running: restarted every time its
run exits, so it never completes on its own". The badge sits inside its
row or card by bounding box, and a one-off proposal next to it has no
badge. Task 20, seeded in the demo, shows the same in the screenshots and
in the contrast pass.

### 2.4 Short phones (#482, §4's drawer identity) — F3, fixed

`ui/e2e/short-phone-layout.spec.js` (new) runs at 390×640 and 360×560 with
touch and `isMobile`:

- **Header bar** (`/inbox`): Open navigation, Settings and New task are all
  on screen, centred on one line (within 4px), and do not overlap. The
  document does not scroll sideways.
- **New chat** (`/session/new`): the chat window is at least 150px tall
  (224px at both sizes). Tap the box, type, and Send enables. The new
  assertion is that Send and the box are both on screen. On the base build
  at 390×640, Send's bottom stayed at 673px (`scrollTop` 0, 49px of
  scroll needed) before and after typing. With the fix it sits at 638px.
- **Drawer**, with a verified IAP identity routed into `/api/config` the
  way `phone-signed-in-logout.spec.js` does: once the slide-in ends, the
  "Signed in as" row and the build stamp are on screen and do not
  overlap. The long email is ellipsized inside the paper. The rail's last
  row (Repos) scrolls into view above the pinned footer, which starts at
  System.

The #482 spec at 320×568 still passes.

## 3. Screenshots

`node e2e/screenshot.mjs --url http://127.0.0.1:18080 --code … --pages
<the 23 pages above> --out screenshots/{before,after}` (the
`make ui-screenshots` script, pointed at the walk's own demo so the seeded
report and proposal are in the shots) shot every page at desktop
(1440×900) and phone (390×844), in light and dark: 92 shots each, plus a
`contact-sheet.png`. The 390×640 New chat and 360×560 drawer shots come
from the same browser at those sizes. `2026-11-03-ui-fixes/fix-sheet.mjs`
crops each fix's before and after side by side.

The screenshots could not be viewed as images in this sandbox (§1). In
their place, `2026-11-03-ui-fixes/layout.mjs` loaded the same 23 pages,
plus `/session/new`, at the same four width × theme combinations (96
loads). For each it recorded whether the document scrolls sideways, and
every visible control or text element cut off by the window's edge
outside a container that scrolls or clips on purpose. Result
(`layout.json`): **0 of 96** with anything cut off. The first version of
the sweep flagged board cards inside `.board-columns`, which scrolls
sideways by design. That was a bug in the sweep, and it is fixed.
